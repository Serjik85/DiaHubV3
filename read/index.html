The Problem We're Solving

  • 📱 Smartphone Dependency: Current solutions require constant phone access, but phones are often unavailable (charging, in bags, during meetings)
  • ⌚ Smartwatch Limitations: Expensive, short battery life, and not suitable for everyone - especially children and elderly users
  • 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Parental Anxiety: Parents need instant, reliable access to their child's glucose levels without complex apps
  • ⚠️ System Failures: Apps crash, notifications fail, and connectivity issues can be life-threatening
  • 💸 Market Gap: No dedicated, affordable, simple solution exists for glanceable glucose monitoring

Our Revolutionary Solution

DiaHub is a compact, dedicated device that receives glucose data remotely through the internet and displays it instantly – no phone or apps required.

✨ Ultra-portable design - fits on any keychain, always accessible

🔋 Weeks of battery life - no daily charging required

📶 Wi-Fi connectivity - simple setup, no apps needed

👀 Instant glance - see glucose levels in under 1 second

💰 Affordable - fraction of the cost of smartwatches

How It Works

  1. Device powers on and creates a Wi‑Fi access point
  2. Connect to Wi‑Fi and link your preferred glucose data source
  3. The device receives and shows glucose in real time
  4. Data updates automatically; ultra‑low power standby

Meet the Founder

About Me

I'm a passionate developer and entrepreneur with a personal connection to diabetes management. Having witnessed the daily challenges faced by people with diabetes and their families, I was inspired to create a solution that makes glucose monitoring more accessible and convenient.

With years of experience in software development and hardware prototyping, I've dedicated myself to building DiaHub - a simple, reliable accessory that brings peace of mind to families managing diabetes.

Background: Software Engineering & Hardware Development
Mission: Making diabetes management effortless and accessible
Vision: A world where glucose monitoring is seamless and stress-free

FAQ

Is this a medical device?

No. DiaHub is a consumer accessory designed to show data at a glance.

Does it require an app?

No. Setup happens via an onboard Wi‑Fi configuration page.

Battery life?

Weeks in standby with periodic updates, depending on usage.

Compatibility?

Works with compatible glucose data sources. No phone or app required.
